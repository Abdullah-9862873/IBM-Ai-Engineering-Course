# Module 1 Quiz: Generative AI and LLMs

## Question 1

Which of the following tasks is the WaveNet generative AI model specifically designed for?

- A) Generating natural-sounding speech and for text-to-speech synthesis
- B) Generating contextually relevant text by understanding the relationship between words and phrases
- C) Generating images based on text input
- D) Creating realistic images from sketches and for deep fake creation

**Answer: A**

---

## Question 2

Identify the generative AI model that primarily utilizes the self-attention mechanism as the training approach.

- A) Variational autoencoders (VAEs)
- B) Transformers
- C) Recurrent neural networks (RNNs)
- D) Generative adversarial networks (GANs)

**Answer: B**

## Question 3

Which of the following large language models (LLMs) uses an encoder-only transformer architecture and is exceptional at understanding the context of a word within a sentence?

- A) Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer (T5)
- B) Generative pre-trained transformer (GPT)
- C) Bidirectional and Auto-Regressive Transformers (BART)
