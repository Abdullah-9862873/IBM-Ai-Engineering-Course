# Answers

1. **The maximum length of the output responses** - `max_seq_length` in SFTConfig controls the maximum length of the input/output sequence during training.

2. **Allow human evaluators to rank responses based on quality** - In reward modeling, human evaluators rank responses (pairwise comparison) which is more reliable than assigning exact numerical scores.

3. **Interprets the differences between good and bad responses** - The sigmoid function σ(r_A - r_B) converts the reward difference into a probability that response A is better than response B.

4. **Applying supervised fine-tuning on task-specific instruction-output datasets** - Instruction-tuning trains models on instruction-output pairs using supervised fine-tuning.

5. **formatting_prompts_func_no_response** - Used for validation without response labels.

6. **By tuning the model's responses based on users' interests** - Reward modeling adjusts responses based on user preferences.

7. **The Bradley-Terry model computes the probability of one item over another using a sigmoid function** - Uses sigmoid to convert reward difference to probability.

8. **trainer.train()** - The RewardTrainer uses trainer.train() to begin training.

9. **To provide the model with structured instruction-response pairs** - Instruction-tuning establishes foundational understanding before RLHF.

10. **get_response** - Organizes prompts and responses for reward modeling.
